⭐What are 'Birds' ?

Birds are a group of warm-blooded vertebrates constituting the class Aves, characterised by feathers, toothless beaked jaws, the laying of hard-shelled eggs, a high metabolic rate, a four-chambered heart, and a strong yet lightweight skeleton. Birds are part of the balance of nearly every ecosystem on the planet. Birds eat insects and plants, and bird eggs are food for may other animals. Fruit-eating birds help spread seeds, and nectar-eating birds pollinate flowers. Birds have long been considered an important part of human culture.


Birds live in a wide variety of habitats across the globe, adapting to diverse environments from dense forests to arid deserts and even urban areas. Some birds, like sparrows and pigeons, thrive near human settlements, while others, like eagles and penguins, inhabit remote areas. Many birds build nests in trees, bushes, or on the ground, providing safe havens for their eggs. Bird habitats are broadly categorized into forest, non-forest, and aquatic areas, each offering unique resources and challenges.

Birds eat a wide variety of foods, with their diet often dictated by their beak shape, habitat, and season. Many birds consume seeds, insects, and fruits, while others specialize in nectar, small animals, or even fish. Some birds, like hummingbirds, are nectarivores, feeding primarily on the sweet liquid from flowers. Birds of prey, such as eagles and hawks, are carnivores, consuming smaller mammals and birds.
